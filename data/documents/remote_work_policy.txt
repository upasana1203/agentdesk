Remote Work Policy

Effective Date: January 2025

All full-time employees are eligible to work remotely up to 3 days per week,
subject to manager approval. Employees choosing a hybrid schedule must be
present in the office on Tuesdays and Thursdays for team collaboration days.

Employees working remotely are expected to be reachable during core hours,
10:00 AM to 4:00 PM local time, and to attend all scheduled meetings with
video on unless otherwise agreed with their manager.

Equipment: the company provides a laptop, monitor, and a one-time $300
home office stipend for remote employees. Requests for additional equipment
should go through the IT helpdesk portal.

International remote work (working from a country other than your home
country) requires prior approval from HR and Legal due to tax and
employment law implications, and is generally limited to 30 days per year.

Fully remote roles (no office attendance requirement) are designated as
such in the job posting and are not subject to the Tuesday/Thursday
in-office requirement.
