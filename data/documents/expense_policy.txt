Travel and Expense Policy

Booking travel
All flights and hotels for business travel must be booked through the
company travel portal to consolidate billing and duty-of-care tracking.
Economy class is standard for flights under 6 hours; premium economy is
permitted for flights over 6 hours with manager approval.

Meal reimbursement
Meals while traveling on company business are reimbursed up to $75/day
(breakfast $15, lunch $20, dinner $40) without itemized receipts required
under $25 per meal. Alcohol is not reimbursable except at client dinners,
up to two drinks per person.

Expense submission
All expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense being
incurred, through the expense portal, with receipts attached for any
single item over $25. Reports submitted after 60 days require VP approval
and may not be reimbursed.

Non-reimbursable items
Personal entertainment, traffic or parking fines, and travel upgrades not
pre-approved by a manager are not reimbursable.
