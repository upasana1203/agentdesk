AgentDesk Store - Frequently Asked Questions

Shipping
Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days within the country of purchase.
Expedited shipping (2-3 business days) is available at checkout for an
additional fee. We do not currently ship internationally.

Returns
Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund,
provided they are unused and in original packaging. Electronics with
opened packaging are subject to a 15% restocking fee. Start a return from
the "Orders" section of your account.

Warranty
All electronics carry a 1-year manufacturer warranty covering defects in
materials and workmanship. This does not cover accidental damage, water
damage, or normal wear and tear. Extended warranty plans are available for
purchase at checkout.

Order changes
Orders can be modified or canceled within 1 hour of placement by contacting
support. After that window, the order has typically entered fulfillment and
cannot be changed; you may still request a return once delivered.

Payment methods
We accept major credit cards, PayPal, and Apple Pay. Purchase orders are
accepted for business accounts with an approved credit line.
