New Employee Onboarding Guide

Week 1: Setup
On your first day, IT will provide your laptop and accounts (email, Slack,
GitHub, and the internal wiki). Complete the mandatory security training
module within your first 3 days -- it takes about 45 minutes.

Week 1: Meet your team
Your manager will schedule 1:1 intro meetings with your immediate team and
key cross-functional partners during your first week. You'll also be
assigned an onboarding buddy, a peer (not your manager) you can ask
"no question is too small" questions.

Week 2-4: Ramp-up projects
New engineers are typically assigned a small, well-scoped starter project
in their first two weeks, meant to touch the main systems they'll work
with without being on the critical path. Non-engineering roles follow an
equivalent shadowing and small-deliverable ramp-up plan set by their
manager.

30/60/90 day check-ins
HR schedules a 30-day, 60-day, and 90-day check-in with you and your
manager to discuss how ramp-up is going, address blockers, and set goals
for the next period. These are separate from performance reviews.

Benefits enrollment
You have 30 days from your start date to enroll in health, dental, and
vision benefits. After 30 days, you'll need to wait for the next open
enrollment period unless you have a qualifying life event.
